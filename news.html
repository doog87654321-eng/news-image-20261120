Weather · India — Lead story

Akvan comes ashore as 100,000 flee India’s east coast

Severe Cyclone Akvan crosses the coast near Gopalpur around noon, with winds of 90–100 kmph gusting to 110. Odisha has moved 105,304 people to 1,224 shelters and put 11 districts on red alert, chasing a zero-casualty landfall.

War · Ukraine

Drones hit two embassies in Kyiv

A day-long Russian drone raid kills one person and damages the Austrian and Dutch embassies and St Volodymyr’s Cathedral. Vienna summons Moscow’s ambassador.

Law · South Korea

Top court sends ex-PM’s case back

The Supreme Court overturns Han Duck-soo’s 15-year sentence over the 2024 martial law declaration and orders a retrial — but upholds his wife Kim Keon Hee’s four-year term.

Diplomacy · Americas

US and Venezuela restore military ties

Defence Secretary Hegseth signs a deal in Caracas for joint training and intelligence sharing, calling Venezuela a “compliant security partner” ten months after Maduro’s capture.

Security · North Korea

IAEA: Pyongyang’s enrichment grows

The UN watchdog says North Korea has finished the exterior of a new extension at its Yongbyon enrichment plant, calling the expanding weapons-grade programme “a cause for serious concern”.

Climate · Pacific

Fiji declares a state of disaster as drought bites

El Niño has driven water shortages to crisis level across the Pacific nation. The government redeploys FJ$20 million for emergency water as forecasters warn of below-normal rain until at least April.
